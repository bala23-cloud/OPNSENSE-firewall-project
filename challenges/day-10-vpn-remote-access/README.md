# Day 10 — VPN Fundamentals and Remote Access

**Guide status:** Documentation complete. Practical validation has not yet been recorded.

## Scenario
A small company needs to strengthen its network security while keeping business services available. This day's lab focuses on vpn fundamentals and remote access.

## Objective
Understand secure remote connectivity into a lab network.

## Lab plan
Compare OpenVPN and IPsec; choose one supported by available lab resources; document authentication, tunnel addressing, routes and access policy before configuration.

## How it will be validated
Validate tunnel establishment, assigned routes, permitted internal access and blocked unauthorized destinations.

## Key lesson
A VPN tunnel being connected does not mean every internal resource should be reachable.
